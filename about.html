Sahil Soft brings e-commerce operations and practical technology together for businesses that want dependable execution—not shortcuts.

Our story

For six years, our virtual assistant team has supported sellers with the daily work that keeps marketplace businesses moving: listings, operations, customer communication, order workflows and measured growth. We work with eBay, Walmart, TikTok Shop, Etsy and Temu across the UK and USA, adapting our approach to the condition, strength and rules of each account. The goal is never reckless volume. It is to help clients build healthier accounts, clearer operations and stronger long-term revenue.

For five years, we have also supplied ready-to-ship inventory to clients in the USA. We understand that a good product is more than a low price: it must be represented honestly, selected with customer satisfaction in mind and ready to move. Our sourcing process focuses on A+ and standard-quality choices, competitive market pricing and products that support sustainable selling.

Technology with accountability

Alongside e-commerce, Sahil Soft has delivered IT services for four to five years. We build premium websites, portfolio sites, mobile applications, AI automations, chatbots, POS systems and custom software. We use AI where it improves speed and research, but we do not hand clients unreviewed one-click output. Every solution is shaped through hands-on coding, thoughtful implementation and human review—so it is stable, purposeful and aligned with the business it represents.
